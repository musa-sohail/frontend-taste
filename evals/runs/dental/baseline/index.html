Your first visit

No surprises. Not in the chair, not on the bill.

  1. A real conversation

    We start with 15 minutes of talking, not drilling: your history, your worries, and what you want from your smile.

  2. Full exam & 3D scan

    Low-dose digital imaging and an intraoral scan you can see on screen, so you understand exactly what we see.

  3. A written plan with prices

    Options ranked by urgency, with your insurance estimate already applied. Take it home, think it over.

  4. Care on your schedule

    Early-morning and Saturday appointments, text reminders, and online rescheduling whenever you need it.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients can usually be seen within a week, and same-day for emergencies. Book online or call us.

How long is a first appointment?

Plan for about 75 minutes. That covers a conversation, full exam, imaging, a cleaning if time allows, and walking you through your treatment plan.

I'm really anxious about the dentist. Can you help?

Very often. Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time, explain each step before we do it, and discuss options like nitrous oxide or oral sedation.

Do you see children?

We see patients of all ages, typically from their first birthday onward. Kids' visits are short, playful and focused on building good habits.

What payment options do you offer?

All major cards, HSA/FSA, and 0% financing for 6–12 months on treatment over $500 through CareCredit or Sunbit.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone else. You can reschedule from the link in your reminder text.
