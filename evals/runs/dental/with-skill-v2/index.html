Questions

Things people ask before booking

I’m in pain right now. Can I be seen today?

Almost certainly. We hold four emergency slots every weekday morning and two on Saturdays. Call (207) 555-0142 and press 1, or pick “Tooth pain” in the booking form above to see today’s openings.

I haven’t been to a dentist in years. Will I be judged?

No. About a third of our new patients haven’t been in five years or more. Your first visit can be a conversation only, and we’ll never start treatment you haven’t agreed to in writing.

Do you see children?

Yes, from the first tooth (usually around age one). Dr. Nandakumar sees most of our kids, and we schedule them after school and on Saturday mornings.

How do payment and insurance work?

We check your benefits before you arrive and text an estimate. You pay your share on the day by card, HSA/FSA, or interest-free monthly payments through Sunbit for balances over $300.

What if I need to cancel?

Just give us 24 hours by text or phone. Late cancellations are $40, waived once a year, and always waived for illness.

Rather talk to a person?

Leave your number and Rosa will call you back within the hour, Monday to Saturday. She can book, check insurance, or just answer a question.
