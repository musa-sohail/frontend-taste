FAQ

Questions teams ask before switching

Something else? Email hello@plotline.app and a real person replies within 4 working hours.

How long does it take to move over from Jira, Asana or Trello?

Most teams are fully moved in under a day. Our importers bring across tasks, assignees, comments, attachments and custom fields. On Business plans, a migration specialist will map your workflows with you.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

You choose a plan or drop to Starter for free, and you keep all your data either way. We never ask for a card to start the trial, so there are no surprise charges.

Do guests and clients need a paid seat?

No. Guests are free and unlimited on every plan. They can view, comment on and complete tasks in the projects you share with them, and nothing else.

Where is our data stored?

In AWS regions in Frankfurt or Virginia, whichever you choose. Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, and we'll sign a DPA on request.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, at any time from the billing page. If you move to annual, we credit the unused part of your current month straight away.
