02 — How it works

From kickoff to shipped in three moves.

Teams are typically up and running in an afternoon. Import from Jira, Asana or a CSV in one click.

Plan the outcome

Write the goal, break it into milestones, and let Tandem suggest a realistic timeline from your team’s past velocity.

STEP 02

Work in the flow

Boards, lists and timelines are just views of the same data. Everyone works the way they like — nobody gets out of sync.

STEP 03

Ship and learn

Automatic reports surface what slipped and why, so every project makes the next one a little smoother.

05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Chat with our team — a real human usually replies within minutes.

Can I import from my current tool?+

Yes. One-click importers for Jira, Asana, Trello, Linear, Monday and ClickUp bring over tasks, comments, attachments and assignees. CSV import covers everything else.

Is the free plan really free?+

Really. Teams of up to 10 can use Tandem’s core features forever, with no credit card and no time limit.

How is my data secured?+

Tandem is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise customers can choose EU or US data residency.

Can I change plans later?+

Anytime. Upgrades apply instantly and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?+

Yes — 50% off the Team plan for verified nonprofits and free Team plans for accredited schools and universities.
