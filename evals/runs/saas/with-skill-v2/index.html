How Baton works

Most delays aren’t in the work. They’re between people.

Teams we interviewed lost an average of 2.4 days per feature waiting on “one quick question” after a handoff. Baton is built to close that gap.

FAQ

Questions teams ask before switching

Something else? Our support team replies in about 2 hours, Monday to Friday. hello@baton.app

How long does it take to move from Jira or Asana?

The importer maps projects, statuses, assignees and comments automatically. Most teams under 5,000 issues are done in under an hour; we’ll run it with you on a call if you’d like.

Do we have to use handoff checklists on every column?

No. They’re off by default. Most teams turn them on for one or two transitions — usually Design → Build and Build → QA — and leave the rest as a normal board.

What counts as an “active member”?

Anyone who creates or edits a task in a billing month. People who only view or comment as guests are free, and we prorate when someone leaves mid-cycle.

Where is our data stored?

In AWS us-east-1 by default, or Frankfurt on the Business plan. Baton is SOC 2 Type II audited and encrypts data at rest with AES-256.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month; annual plans are refunded pro rata in the first 60 days. You can export everything as CSV or JSON whenever you want.
