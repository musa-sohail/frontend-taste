Our Approach

Rigor and wonder, side by side.

We believe children learn best when they're known well, challenged thoughtfully, and given real work that matters to them.

Inquiry first

Units start with a question worth asking — why does the creek flood each spring? — and students build the knowledge to answer it.

Known by name

Every child has an advisor who knows their strengths, their stretches, and their favorite book. Families are partners, not spectators.

Outside, often

Our garden, trails, and outdoor classrooms are part of the curriculum. Kids measure, plant, observe, and get muddy every week.

Academic Programs

A path that grows with your child.

Three divisions, one continuous philosophy — each designed around what children need at that stage of life.

Kindergarten – Grade 4

Lower School

Strong foundations in reading, writing, and math, taught through play, hands-on projects, and plenty of time outdoors. Children learn to ask good questions and to be good friends.

  • Structured literacy program
  • Singapore-inspired math
  • Weekly garden science
  • Spanish from Kindergarten
  • Music, art & movement daily
  • Social-emotional curriculum

Grades 5 – 8

Middle School

Students take on more independence, deeper content, and real responsibility. Advisory groups, interdisciplinary projects, and leadership roles prepare them for high school and beyond.

  • Pre-algebra through Algebra I
  • Lab sciences & robotics
  • Humanities seminar
  • Annual outdoor expedition
  • 8th grade capstone project
  • High school placement support

Admissions 2027–28

Let's get to know each other.

Our admissions process is simple, personal, and focused on finding the right fit for your child. Priority deadline: January 15.

  1. Inquire & visit

    Tell us about your family and join a campus tour or Open House to see classes in action.

    September – January

  2. Apply

    Submit the online application, a parent questionnaire, and recent school records.

    Priority deadline · Jan 15

  3. Visit day

    Your child spends a morning with future classmates while you meet with our admissions team.

    January – February

  4. Decisions

    Families receive admission and tuition assistance decisions together, with time to decide.
