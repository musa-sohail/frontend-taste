Our approach

Rigorous without the rush

We trade worksheets for real work: water-quality data handed to the city, a novel printed on our own press, a bridge that has to hold a teacher.

  • Place-based learning

    The Linden Woods, Reed Creek and our 2-acre farm are part of the curriculum in every grade.

  • Advisors who stay

    One advisor follows each student through a division and meets their family every term.

  • Writing everywhere

    Students write weekly in every subject, including math and science, with one-on-one feedback.

  • Character in practice

    40 service hours before graduation, much of it with our neighbors at Manna FoodBank.

Admissions for 2027–28

Four steps, and a real person at each one

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis after January 15. Need-based aid is available at every grade; last year 34% of families received it.

Questions parents ask

Good to know before you visit

Something else? Call Ellen Marsh in admissions at (828) 555-0142.

What does tuition cost for 2027–28?

Tuition ranges from $16,400 (Pre-K) to $27,900 (Upper School) and includes lunch, books, laptops from Grade 6, and all expedition trips. Monthly payment plans are available.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. We awarded $2.4M in need-based aid last year, and 34% of families received a grant. Aid applications go through Clarity and are due February 1.

Do you offer transportation?

Three morning and afternoon bus routes serve West Asheville, Black Mountain and Weaverville. Extended day runs until 5:45pm for Pre-K through Grade 8.

How does Linden Hill support different learners?

Our Learning Center has five specialists who work with students one-on-one or in small groups, and coordinate accommodations with teachers and families.

Can my child spend a day at school before applying?

Absolutely. Shadow days are available Tuesday through Thursday from October to February for applicants in Grades 3–11.
