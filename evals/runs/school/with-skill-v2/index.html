Our approach

Rigour that still leaves room to wonder.

We ask a lot of our students. We also make sure every one of them has an adult who notices when they need something different.

  • An advisor for every student

    From Grade 6, groups of nine meet daily with one teacher who follows them for three years.

  • Twenty-two acres of classroom

    Our creek, orchard and forest trail are used weekly in science, art and writing, in every season.

  • Progress you can actually read

    Narrative reports three times a year, written by teachers, not generated from a grade book.

Questions parents ask

The honest answers.

Still wondering? Call admissions on (503) 555-0192.

What does tuition cost for 2027–28?

Tuition is $24,800 for Early Years, $31,600 for Grades 1–5 and $36,900 for Grades 6–12, including lunch, books and outdoor trips. 38% of families receive need-based aid, with an average award of $19,400.

Is there school transport?

Four bus routes cover Lake Oswego, Beaverton, the West Hills and inner Southeast, plus a free shuttle from the Sunset TriMet transit centre.

How do you support students who learn differently?

Our Learning Center has six specialists who work with teachers and families on accommodations, executive-function coaching and testing arrangements, at no extra cost.

Can my child shadow for a day before applying?

Yes. Applicants for Grades 3–12 spend a visit day with a student host between November and February. Younger applicants join a small-group play morning instead.

What happens before and after school?

Campus opens at 7:15 am. After-school care runs until 6:00 pm for Pre-K to Grade 5, and the library stays open until 5:30 pm for older students.
