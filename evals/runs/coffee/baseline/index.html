Our story

A corner café that grew a roaster.

Halfmoon started in 2016 as a six-seat espresso bar run by two former baristas who wanted a place that felt like a friend's kitchen. The neighbors kept showing up, so we kept adding chairs.

Today we roast our own beans, bake every morning and host open-mic nights on the first Friday of the month. The coffee got better. The kitchen-table feeling stayed.

Direct trade

We know our growers by name and visit every harvest we can.

Low waste

Compostable cups, refill discounts and grounds free for gardeners.

Paid fairly

Every barista earns a living wage plus tips and health coverage.

Stay a while

Fast Wi-Fi, plenty of outlets and no laptop curfew. Really.
