Our story

A 5-kilo roaster, a crow named Juniper, and a lot of early mornings

Maya and Tomás Okafor-Reyes opened Kettle & Crow in a former bike shop in March 2017.

Tomás had spent six years roasting for others; Maya ran the floor at a Seattle café. They bought a second-hand Diedrich IR-5, painted the walls oat, and started roasting in the back room before opening each morning.

Juniper, the crow, showed up the first week and never left. She gets a peanut at 7 am. We buy from the same four farms year after year and publish what we pay them on every bag.

Good to know

Questions we get at the counter

Is there Wi-Fi, and can I work here?

Yes: free Wi-Fi, outlets along the window bar. We ask laptops to make room for the weekend rush, so the big table is laptop-free 9 am–1 pm on Saturdays and Sundays.

Are dogs welcome?

On the patio, always, with a water bowl by the door. Inside we can only allow service animals.

Do you ship beans?

Anywhere in the US for a flat $5, free over $40. Orders placed by Monday night ship Wednesday, roasted the day before.

Can you cater our office?

We do coffee boxes (serves 10–12) for $38 with 24 hours’ notice, and full espresso carts for events. Email hello@kettleandcrow.coffee.

How do I join the Saturday cupping?

It’s free and first-come: eight seats at 2 pm. Put your name on the chalkboard by the register any time that week.
