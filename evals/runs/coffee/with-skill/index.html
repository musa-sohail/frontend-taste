Our story

A 5-kilo roaster, a borrowed corner, and a lot of early mornings

Halfmoon started in the back of Rosa's bike shop with a secondhand Diedrich roaster and three wholesale accounts. Eleven years later we have our own room on Division, but the roaster is the same one — it just has more stickers now.

We buy from fourteen farms, most of them for five seasons or longer, and pay well above the Fair Trade floor because good coffee costs what it costs. Everything we serve was roasted within the last 10 days.

MO

Maya Okafor
Founder & head roaster

From farm to cup

Every bag passes through four pairs of hands we know by name

  1. Sourced direct

    We visit partner farms every other year and cup samples from each harvest before committing.

  2. Roasted Tue & Fri

    Small 5 kg batches, profiled by Maya to taste like the place it grew — not like the roast.

  3. Rested 4 days

    Fresh isn't always best. Espresso rests before it hits the grinder, so it pulls sweet and even.

  4. Poured by hand

    Our baristas dial in every morning at 6am. If a shot runs fast, it goes down the drain.

Good to know

Questions we hear at the counter

Can I work from the café?

Absolutely. Wi-Fi is free and fast, and the window bar has outlets. On weekends between 9am and 1pm we ask laptop folks to share tables so everyone gets a seat.

Do you have dairy-free and gluten-free options?

Oat and almond milk are free with any drink. Our olive-oil cake is gluten-free, though it's baked in a kitchen that also uses wheat flour.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes — we roast for about thirty cafés, restaurants and offices around Portland. Email wholesale@halfmooncoffee.com and we'll set up a tasting.

Can I book the space for an event?

After 5pm the café is available for small gatherings of up to 30 people, with coffee, tea and pastries. Public cupping classes run the first Saturday of every month at 3pm ($25).
